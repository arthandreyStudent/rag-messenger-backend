[Company Profile]

    NexusTech was founded in 2015 by a group of engineers and business professionals who believed that cloud computing and artificial intelligence would transform the way organizations handle data. Over the years, NexusTech has grown from a small consulting practice into a regional IT solutions provider with a strong focus on cloud migration, cybersecurity, and data-driven analytics. The company’s mission has always been to make complex technologies accessible, reliable, and ethically sound.

    From its Singapore headquarters, NexusTech has expanded operations to key markets in Manila and Jakarta, with growing client engagement in Cebu, Philippines. Cebu has been identified as a future growth hub due to its strong pool of IT talent, its role as a BPO capital, and its ecosystem of SMEs looking for digital transformation solutions. NexusTech continues to invest in regional hubs that combine global expertise with local market understanding.

    Today, NexusTech employs more than 500 professionals across multiple disciplines—cloud architects, cybersecurity engineers, data scientists, software developers, and project managers—working together to deliver end-to-end IT solutions.
